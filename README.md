# Toyota Global Vehicle Sales Performance (2019–2025)

## Project Overview
This project presents a descriptive analytics report on Toyota vehicle sales performance from 2019 to 2025. It analyzes best-selling models, regional demand, customer ratings, pricing differences, revenue, and profit to identify trends and important business insights.

**Note:** Toyota's live transaction data is not publicly accessible. This project uses a synthetic dataset designed to represent general automobile industry patterns. The results are illustrative and are not official Toyota sales or financial figures.

## Objectives
- Identify the best-selling Toyota models.
- Analyze yearly sales trends from 2019 to 2025.
- Compare regional demand across India, America, Japan, and Germany.
- Analyze average on-road prices and ex-showroom prices.
- Compare profit per unit across regions.
- Study customer ratings by model and year.
- Identify the highest-revenue models and model-region combinations.
- Provide recommendations based on the analysis.

## Dataset Information
- **Time Period:** 2019–2025
- **Dataset Size:** Approximately 300,000 records
- **Regions:** India, America, Japan, Germany
- **Vehicle Models:** 20 Toyota models
- **Key Metrics:** Units sold, revenue, profit, prices, discounts, and customer ratings
- **File Formats:** CSV and Excel

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas

- Jupyter Notebook
- CSV and Excel
- Data Visualization and Descriptive Analytics

## Project Workflow
1. Load and inspect the dataset.
2. Check missing values, data types, and data distributions.
3. Calculate summary statistics and key performance metrics.
4. Analyze sales by model, region, and year.
5. Compare revenue, profit, prices, and discounts.
6. Study customer ratings and their trends.
7. Create charts and visualizations.
8. Summarize findings and provide business recommendations.

## Key Findings

- **Best-Selling Models:** Etios and Corolla are the leading models by recorded sales volume.
- **Highest-Revenue Model:** Land Cruiser is the leading revenue contributor in the report.
- **Sales Growth:** Recorded annual sales increase throughout the 2019–2025 period, with 2025 being the strongest year.
- **Regional Demand:** India contributes approximately 40% of the dataset's recorded units.
- **Regional Profitability:** America has the highest reported average profit per unit, while India has the lowest.
- **Customer Ratings:** The overall average customer rating is approximately 3.85 out of 5.
- **Revenue Contribution:** Land Cruiser in India is identified as the largest model-region revenue combination.
- **Discount Analysis:** The median discount is lower than the mean, suggesting that some large discounts increase the average.

## Recommendations
- Review pricing, discounts, and model mix in India to understand its lower average profit per unit.
- Monitor the Land Cruiser–India combination because of its substantial revenue contribution.
- Track customer ratings regularly to identify potential satisfaction issues.
- Compare pricing and profitability patterns across all four regions.
- Investigate lower-rated models, including Avalon.
- Use validated historical data for future sales and revenue forecasting.

## Future Scope
The project can be extended from descriptive analytics to predictive analytics by:
- Forecasting future vehicle sales by model and region.
- Predicting revenue using historical sales, price, and discount data.
- Estimating regional demand.
- Analyzing possible changes in customer ratings.
- Developing an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Limitations
- The dataset is synthetic and does not represent verified official Toyota transactions.
- Results depend on the assumptions used to create the dataset.
- Revenue and profit figures are illustrative rather than audited financial results.
- Regional comparisons may be affected by currency, taxes, and vehicle-model mix.
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish cause-and-effect relationships.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how descriptive analytics can be used to understand vehicle sales patterns, compare regional performance, evaluate customer ratings, and identify revenue and profitability trends. It also provides a foundation for future predictive analytics and data-driven business decision-making.
